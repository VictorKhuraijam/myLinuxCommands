pw-loopback -m "Echo-cancelled source"

Acts like a real-time monitor for your microphone. It intercepts the processed audio coming out of your clean, echo-cancelled
virtual microphone feed and routes it directly back into your own speakers or headphones so you can hear exactly what you sound
like in real-time.
Breaking down exactly how it works piece by piece:
• pw-loopback: This is a built-in PipeWire utility. Its sole job is to create a digital "bridge" between an audio input
device (like a microphone) and an audio output device (like your speakers or headphones).
• -m (or --menu): This flag instructs PipeWire to route the audio explicitly using a named media role or a specific targeted
profile mapping.
• "Echo-cancelled source": This is the exact name of the clean, filtered virtual microphone filter we configured back in Step 1.

Why we use it for testing

Normally, you can't hear your own microphone unless you are actively recorded or inside an official call app. Running
this command lets you safely test your microphone's clarity, volume balance, and background noise level locally on your Arch
machine before hopping onto a real video call with another person.
Note: Remember to close it out by pressing Ctrl + C in your terminal when you are done testing, or you will continue to
hear your own voice over your speakers!
