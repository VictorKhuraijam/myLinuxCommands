systemctl --user restart pipewire pulse:
   This only flushes and restarts your audio streams. Your screen will not flicker.
   The only side effect is that any audio currently playing will pause or mute for about 1 to 2 seconds while
   the sound card reinitializes.

sudo systemctl restart NetworkManager:
   This only restarts the background service managing your internet connections. Your display is completely safe.
   The only side effect is that your Wi-Fi or Ethernet will disconnect for about 3 to 5 seconds before automatically reconnecting.
